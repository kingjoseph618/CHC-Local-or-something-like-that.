Case Note 001 // Partnership Outreach Proposal

KINGSMAN NFP AI TRAINING
& LEGAL AID OUTREACH.

Lead entity: Kingsman NFP
Proposed outreach target: Land of Lincoln Legal Aid, East St. Louis

1. Mission Objective

Explore a collaborative, faith-informed outreach model providing litigation-support and educational resources for people with criminal backgrounds, including pro se litigants. The proposed pilot would evaluate whether AI-assisted investigation, discovery review, research organization, and document administration can reduce appropriate administrative workload for participating attorneys.

Status clarification: This is a Kingsman proposal and outreach target. It does not state or imply that Land of Lincoln Legal Aid has accepted, endorsed, signed, or partnered in the project. Any MOU remains a draft until every named party executes it.

Methodology

The Investigator Model

Analytical depth inspired by a fictional master investigator: thorough, evidence-focused, mission-driven, and disciplined about uncertainty.

Primary functions: discovery analysis, statutory-research organization, chronology building, and document indexing.

Operational Boundary

Support, Not Representation

Outputs are categorized as litigation support, administrative assistance, or educational resources. Bots do not establish an attorney-client relationship, give autonomous legal advice, or replace licensed counsel.

Partnership Ethos

One Hand Helps the Other

Open collaboration may include appropriate observers from legal services, the judiciary, law enforcement, faith communities, and the public—subject to privacy, privilege, informed consent, and counsel direction.

Case Note 002 // Legal-Technology Workstreams

THREE TRACKS.
ONE COUNSEL-LED RECORD.

Track 01 — Systemic Pattern Review

Evaluate whether approved technical records reveal recurring operational-friction or instruction-compliance patterns. Confirm counsel approval and secure storage first; analyze working copies rather than sole originals; preserve source links; and never treat an AI pattern as proof of fault, intent, motive, liability, or damages.

Track 02 — Chronology & Litigation Processing

Organize counsel-approved transcripts, scripts, records, and correspondence into a draft chronology covering July 2–August 14, 2026. Human reviewers must separate documented facts, participant statements, AI interpretations, allegations, and unknowns.

Track 03 — Human Counsel & Legal-Aid Delivery

Confirm jurisdiction, eligibility, conflicts, deadlines, and accepted delivery method. Use secure devices and official channels. Provide a lawyer-reviewed executive summary and evidence manifest; transmit sensitive originals only through counsel-approved secure systems.

Case Note 003 // Recorder Assessment

NEXT AUTHORIZED
STEPS.

  1. Freeze the source set. Preserve originals, metadata, Drive IDs, timestamps, hashes, terminal output, and revision history.
  2. Let counsel define scope. Confirm parties, legal terms, deadlines, viable theories, notices, and privilege handling.
  3. Create one evidence manifest. Record item ID, custodian, date, source, hash, supported fact, and privilege or redaction status.
  4. Redact before public release. Remove credentials, private medical or family details, financial data, client or student information, and precise location data.
  5. Validate every tool. Confirm subscriptions, actual platform functions, data-use terms, export options, and retention rules before uploading evidence.
  6. Measure loss conservatively. Separate user calculations from documented expenses, lost income, causation evidence, and legally recoverable damages.
